Here's how to feel a game's DualSense haptics on a controller that only rumbles. You're hearing a generated voice.

This PlayStation controller has an Xbox Series X Controller assigned. Open it. Its preset is DualSense (PS5): Full, the default, and the one that carries a game's haptics.

Games that use the DualSense's haptics play them as sound, and send no rumble. PadForge turns that sound's loudness into rumble on the Xbox pad: the left channel drives the left motor, and the right channel the right.

On the Force Feedback tab, Overall Gain sets how strong it feels. Zero turns it off for this pad.

There's nothing else to set. No game ran for this video.
