Here's how to set a DualSense's adaptive triggers, lightbar, and speaker in PadForge. You're hearing a generated voice.

Open the controller, and go to the Adaptive Triggers tab.

Pick an effect for each trigger. Weapon is a soft pull into a firm click, like a gun.

Range sets where the effect starts and ends. Strength sets how hard it pushes back.

On the Lighting tab, Mode sets the lightbar. Rainbow Cycle turns it through every color.

On the Audio tab, tick Mirror System Audio, and the PC's sound plays from the pad's speaker.

A game that drives the triggers or the lightbar itself takes over while it runs. Pick up the pad to feel it.
