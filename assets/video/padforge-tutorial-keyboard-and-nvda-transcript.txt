Here's how to set up a controller in PadForge from the keyboard, with NVDA reading along. You're hearing a generated voice, and NVDA.

PadForge runs as administrator, so use the installed copy of NVDA. A portable copy reads PadForge only when it runs as administrator too.

On the Dashboard, Tab moves to Add Controller. Enter opens its list, and Enter again adds an Xbox 360 controller.

NVDA: Profiles, data item, 2 of 4

NVDA: Devices, data item, 3 of 4

NVDA: list Add Controller, data item, 1 of 1 Add Controller, button

NVDA: Xbox, button

NVDA: PadForge, window

Tab on to Devices, and press Enter. Tab through the list to your controller, and Space picks it.

NVDA: Navigation Pane, toggle button, pressed

NVDA: Full Screen, toggle button, not pressed

NVDA: Devices, data item, 3 of 5

NVDA: Slot 1, data item, 4 of 5

NVDA: ALL, list DualSense Wireless Controller [Online], not selected, 1 of 15

NVDA: selected

Tab to the slot button marked 1, and Space assigns the pad to Slot 1.

NVDA: list 1, data item, 1 of 1 1, toggle button, not pressed, Toggle assignment

NVDA: pressed Assigned "DualSense Wireless Controller" to Virtual Controller 1.

In the sidebar, Tab to Slot 1, and Enter opens its page. Then Tab to the Mappings tab, and press Space.

NVDA: Slot 1, data item, 4 of 5

NVDA: Mappings, radio button, not checked

Tab reaches each row of the table, and NVDA reads its output and its source. Enter opens a row's settings, and Enter again folds them away.

NVDA: Mappings, data grid A ← A, data item, 1 of 22

NVDA: B ← B, data item, 2 of 22

NVDA: X ← X, data item, 3 of 22

NVDA: selected

On a row, the arrow keys and letters do nothing, so a key your keyboard maps never changes the table.

NVDA: X
