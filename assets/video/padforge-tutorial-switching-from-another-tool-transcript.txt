Here's how to move to PadForge from Steam Input, DS4Windows, reWASD, or SimHub, one game at a time. You're hearing a generated voice.

First, back up. On the Profiles page, Export saves the profile you're working on.

Coming from Steam Input? Browse Community Configs imports a Workshop layout.

Give every input one owner. Stop the old tool for that game, and in Settings, let HidHide hide the real pad from games.

Then rebuild in layers: the controller type, then the mappings, then layers and macros. Test each one in the game before the next.

Keep the old tool and its setup until PadForge does everything the game needs.

Find more guides at padforge.org.
