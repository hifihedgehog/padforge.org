Here's how to make a macro in PadForge. This one saves a screenshot when you click the DualSense's touchpad. You're hearing a generated voice.

Open the controller, go to the Macros tab, and click Add.

Name it Screenshot.

Under Trigger, open Add from List. Below the Any Device group, the DualSense has its own inputs. Pick Touchpad Click.

Consume Trigger Buttons is on, so the game never sees that click. Fire stays On Press: one click, one run.

Click Add Action, and change its type to Key Press.

In Key Combo, type each key's name in curly braces: LWin, then PrintScreen.

The macro saves as you go. Click the touchpad, and Windows saves a screenshot to your Pictures folder.
