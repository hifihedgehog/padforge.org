Here's how to change what a button does, quiet a drifting stick, and make a trigger reach full sooner. You're hearing a generated voice.

Open your controller's page, then its Mappings tab. Each row is one output the game sees, and A comes first.

To make your pad's B button drive A, click the A row, open its Source list, and pick B. It takes effect at once.

Do the same on the B row, and pick A. The swap is done, and there is no save step.

With the pad in hand, the record button is quicker. Click it, then press the button you want.

On the Sticks tab, raise Deadzone X and Y a little, to ignore a drifting stick. The circle beside them shows the stick before and after.

On the Triggers tab, pull the right end of Range in, and the trigger reads full before it bottoms out.

Each change works at once, and PadForge saves it for you. Find more guides at padforge.org.
