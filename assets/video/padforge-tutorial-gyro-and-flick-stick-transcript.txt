Here's how to aim with gyro and Flick Stick in PadForge. You're hearing a generated voice.

This Keyboard + Mouse controller has a DualSense assigned. Open it, and go to the Gyro tab.

Put the controller down, hands off, and click Calibrate Gyro. The live readout should then sit near zero.

Below it, Sensitivity sets how far the pointer moves for each turn of the pad.

On the Mappings tab, click the Mouse X row, and open its source list.

Scroll to Flick Stick, for the right stick, and pick it.

Then click the Mouse Y row, and open its list.

Gyro Pitch sits right after the touchpad inputs, near the end of the list. Pick it.

Now the stick turns you left and right, and tilting the pad aims up and down.

On the Sticks tab, scroll to the end of the page. Flick Stick has a card of its own there, and its Flick Time sets how long a turn takes.

Nothing moves while the pad sits on the desk. Pick it up, flick the right stick to turn, and tilt the pad to aim.
