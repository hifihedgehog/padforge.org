Here's how to turn a phone, or anything with a web browser, into a controller for PadForge. You're hearing a generated voice.

On the Dashboard, tick Enable Web Controller Server. The card then shows an address, and a QR code.

Scan the code with your phone's camera, or open the address in any browser on the same network. Here, it's a browser on this PC.

PadForge signs its own certificate, so the first visit shows a warning. Click Advanced, then Continue. The browser remembers the choice.

Pick a layout. This is the Xbox 360 one. The page reloads once, and the controller appears.

In PadForge, the Devices page lists the browser as an Xbox 360 web controller. Click it, and assign it to the virtual controller.

Now drag the stick in the browser. Its axes move in PadForge, as a real controller's would.

On a phone, the layout needs landscape. Add the page to your home screen to hide the address bar.
