Here's how to make a DualSense look like an Xbox controller to your games. You're hearing a generated voice.

On the Dashboard, click Add Controller, and pick Xbox.

Turn on your DualSense, by cable or Bluetooth, and open the Devices page. Drag the DualSense onto the new controller in the sidebar.

PadForge knows the DualSense, so it maps it for you. Open the controller's Mappings tab. Cross drives A, Circle drives B, Square X, and Triangle Y. The sticks, triggers and D-pad go where an Xbox pad has them.

Games now see an Xbox controller, and show Xbox prompts. A game's rumble still shakes the DualSense.

If a game also sees the DualSense itself, tick Hide from Games on its panel in Devices. Once HidHide is installed, PadForge ticks it for you.

That's it. Find more guides at padforge.org.
