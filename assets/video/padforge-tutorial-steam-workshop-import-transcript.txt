Here's how to bring a Steam Input layout from the Steam Workshop into PadForge. You're hearing a generated voice.

First, open Settings, and tick Enable Community Configs. PadForge then contacts Steam's servers only when you search, open a config, or check for updates.

On the Profiles page, click Browse Community Configs, and search for your game.

Its configs are listed by rating. A chip narrows them to your controller. Open one to read its translation manifest.

Clean rows carry over as they are. Partial ones work with a difference. Skipped ones are left out, and each row says why.

Click Save Profile. The new profile has no controller yet, so assign yours, and link the game's program.
