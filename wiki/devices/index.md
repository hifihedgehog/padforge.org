# Devices

Most controllers work in PadForge with no special setup: plug in, assign to
a slot, play. The pages in this section exist for the families that have
extra capabilities or extra setup steps worth their own walkthrough.

| Family | Why it has a page |
| --- | --- |
| [DualShock 3](dualshock-3.md) | Needs a one-time pairing step over USB before Bluetooth works |
| [Wii Controllers](wii-controllers.md) | Pairing, extensions, Motion Plus, and the IR pointer |

Families without a page here (DualSense, DualShock 4, Xbox, Switch Pro,
Joy-Con, Steam Controller, Steam Deck, generic DirectInput) are covered by
the [Features](../features/index.md) pages, because everything about them is
standard: their capabilities light up automatically wherever they apply.
