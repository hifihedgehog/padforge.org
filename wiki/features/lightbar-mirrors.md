# Lightbar Mirrors and Sensa Haptics

The Dashboard's Lightbar Mirrors and Razer Sensa HD Haptics sections are gone. The Razer Chroma, Logitech LIGHTSYNC and Razer Sensa rows on the Devices page replace them, and each takes the lighting or rumble of the virtual controller you assign it to. PadForge turns a switch that was on into an assignment: a mirror's row goes to the first DualSense, DualSense Edge or DualShock 4 virtual controller, if there is one, and in the x64 build the Sensa row goes to the lowest-numbered virtual controller. See [Mice, Keyboards and Vendor Rows](peripherals.md).
