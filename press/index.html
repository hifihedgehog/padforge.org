Release notes ↗

PadForge 5.1.0

What's new

  • 109 languagesThe interface comes in 109 languages, 99 of them new and 42 complete. Arabic, Persian, Urdu and Central Kurdish lay it out right to left, and Chinese, Japanese and Korean text draws in its own font.
  • Screen readers and the keyboardPadForge is tested with NVDA. Every control the Tab key reaches has a spoken name in the interface's language, status changes are spoken as they happen, and chips, tiles and dialogs work from the keyboard. The app looks as it did.
  • Lighting for mice and keyboardsA mouse or keyboard assigned to a virtual controller takes that controller's lighting, with the modes of a DualSense lightbar. Logitech, Razer and SteelSeries devices light directly while their software is closed, and through it while it runs.
  • Rumble in a mouseThe Logitech MX Master 4 and the SteelSeries Rival 500, 700 and 710 take their virtual controller's rumble, as do the Logitech iFeel mice on PadForge's driver. The Razer Sensa row carries it to Sensa HD gear.
  • Virtual keyboards and miceA Keyboard + Mouse slot can type and point through one of 51 keyboard and 52 mouse models that programs see as that hardware.
  • HIDMaestro 2.0PadForge moves to HIDMaestro 2.0.3 and its 337 device profiles. Settings removes HIDMaestro, and the PlayStation Bluetooth drivers, after a preview of what each removal takes.
  • 1,109 devicesPadForge knows 1,109 devices by USB identity, the PS VR Aim Controller among them, and the NYXI Hyperion 3 and console-protocol Joy-Con 2 clones connect over Bluetooth.

The project

About PadForge

PadForge is a free controller remapper for Windows. It maps gamepads, wheels, flight sticks, keyboards and mice onto as many as 16 virtual controllers, and it knows 1,109 devices by their USB identity. Players can tune stick response and gyro aiming, build macros and save profiles for individual games. PadForge also imports supported Steam community layouts and connects browser-based controllers. Its source code is available under CC BY-NC-SA 4.0.
