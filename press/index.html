About PadForge

PadForge is a free controller remapper for Windows. It maps gamepads, wheels, flight sticks, keyboards, and mice to virtual controllers. Players can tune stick response and gyro aiming, build macros, and save profiles for individual games. PadForge also imports supported Steam community layouts and connects browser-based controllers. Its source code is available under CC BY-NC-SA 4.0.
