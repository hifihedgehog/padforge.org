PadForge
Product information | October 23, 2026

PadForge is a free controller remapper for Windows. It maps gamepads, wheels, flight sticks, keyboards and mice onto as many as 16 virtual controllers, and it knows 1,109 devices by their USB identity. Players can tune stick response and gyro aiming, build macros and save profiles for individual games. PadForge also imports supported Steam community layouts and connects browser-based controllers. Its source code is available under CC BY-NC-SA 4.0.

New in 5.1.0
109 languages: The interface comes in 109 languages, 99 of them new and 42 complete, with British English beside American English. Arabic, Persian, Urdu and Central Kurdish lay it out right to left, and Chinese, Japanese and Korean text draws in its own font.
Screen readers and the keyboard: PadForge is tested with NVDA. Every control the Tab key reaches has a spoken name in the interface's language, status changes are spoken as they happen, and chips, tiles and dialogs work from the keyboard. The app looks as it did.
Lighting for mice and keyboards: A mouse or keyboard assigned to a virtual controller takes that controller's lighting, with the modes of a DualSense lightbar. Logitech, Razer and SteelSeries devices light directly while their software is closed, and through it while it runs. Vendor rows carry the lighting to headsets and mousepads.
Rumble in a mouse: The Logitech MX Master 4 and the SteelSeries Rival 500, 700 and 710 take their virtual controller's rumble, as do the Logitech iFeel mice on PadForge's driver. The Razer Sensa row carries it to Sensa HD gear.
Virtual keyboards and mice: A Keyboard + Mouse slot can type and point through one of 51 keyboard and 52 mouse models that programs see as that hardware.
HIDMaestro 2.0: PadForge moves to HIDMaestro 2.0.3 and its 337 device profiles. Settings removes HIDMaestro, and the PlayStation Bluetooth drivers, after a preview of what each removal takes.
1,109 devices: PadForge knows 1,109 devices by USB identity, the PS VR Aim Controller among them, and the NYXI Hyperion 3 and console-protocol Joy-Con 2 clones connect over Bluetooth.

Setup
PadForge runs as administrator. Its runtime is included in the executable.
HIDMaestro provides virtual controller output and is installed when needed.
Composite USB profiles install the USB/IP driver on first use.
PadForge 4.5.3 and later update themselves from the Updates card in Settings.
Installation and removal: https://padforge.org/docs/start/installation/

Notes
The direct lighting and rumble paths for mice and keyboards follow the
public implementations that drive each device, and tests check every frame
against them. They have not run on the devices themselves.
No native speaker has reviewed the translations. A partial translation
shows English wherever it has no string.
No NYXI Hyperion 3 has run against PadForge.
DualSense audio over Bluetooth uses a virtual USB DualSense for compatible
games. The game supplies its native speaker audio and haptic effects.
Steam imports include a translation report. Some bindings are approximated
or skipped. Game-specific Steam Input API actions do not translate.
The ARM64 build has not run on ARM64 hardware.
