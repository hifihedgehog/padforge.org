PadForge
Product information | October 3, 2026

PadForge is a free controller remapper for Windows. It maps gamepads, wheels, flight sticks, keyboards and mice onto as many as 16 virtual controllers, and it knows 1,108 devices by their USB identity. Players can tune stick response and gyro aiming, build macros and save profiles for individual games. PadForge also imports supported Steam community layouts and connects browser-based controllers. Its source code is available under CC BY-NC-SA 4.0.

New in 5.0.0
Analog keyboards: PadForge reads how far each key of a Hall effect or analog keyboard is pressed, on more than 370 models from 101 brands. A key can drive a trigger, one side of a stick, or two macros at different depths.
Rapid Trigger and Toggle: Rapid Trigger presses and releases on movement instead of at one fixed point, and it works on any input with depth. Toggle holds an output on with one press and lets go with the next.
Bliss-Box adapters: Each adapter port names the console controller plugged into it and maps it the way SDL or RetroArch does. PadForge reads DualShock 2 button pressure, drives rumble, puts pictures on a Dreamcast VMU and backs up N64 Controller Paks.
Wii Remote light gun: A Wii Remote calibrates to the screen in four shots and then aims where it points. A Namco GunCon 2 calibrates the same way against a CRT.
Motion from a stick or a key: PlayStation, Nintendo and Valve slots gain Motion Pitch, Yaw and Roll rows, so a stick, a trigger or a key can turn the virtual controller's gyro.
DualShock 3: The pad draws as itself in 3D and 2D, and it can fill pitch and roll from its accelerometer. A new preset presents button pressure in the form PCSX2 and RPCS3 read.
1,108 devices: PadForge knows 1,108 devices by USB identity, up from 758 in 4.5.3. It reads Bluetooth LE, serial and other controllers that give Windows no usable input, and installs the driver some of them need.

Setup
PadForge runs as administrator. Its runtime is included in the executable.
HIDMaestro provides virtual controller output and is installed when needed.
Composite USB profiles install the USB/IP driver on first use.
PadForge 4.5.3 and later update themselves from the Updates card in Settings.
Installation and removal: https://padforge.org/docs/start/installation/

Notes
DualSense audio over Bluetooth uses a virtual USB DualSense for compatible
games. The game supplies its native speaker audio and haptic effects.
Steam imports include a translation report. Some bindings are approximated
or skipped. Game-specific Steam Input API actions do not translate.
Most hardware new in 5.0.0 has not been tested on real devices, including
analog keyboards, Bliss-Box adapters and the Wii Remote light gun. Each
reader follows the public implementations that drive the device, and tests
check it against their byte formats. The ARM64 build has not run on ARM64
hardware.
